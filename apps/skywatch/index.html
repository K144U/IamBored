    This circle is your sky

    The middle is straight above you and the edge is the horizon, with north at the top. Green dots are satellites you can see right now; pink ones are the Moon and planets; white ones are stars.

    Tap any dot to see what it is and where to look.

    📷

    Point your phone at a light

    Seen a light and wondering what it is? Tap 📷 Point at the sky and hold your phone up like you're taking its photo. SkyWatch names it.

    If the guesses seem off, aim at the Moon or a bright planet and tap 🧭 Fix compass.

    🛰️

    Catch the space station

    Passes lists when the ISS and other bright satellites cross your sky after dark, with a countdown. Tap 📅 to put one in your calendar.

    First, tell SkyWatch where you are in Settings (or with the button on the sky map).
