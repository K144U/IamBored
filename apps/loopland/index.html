Getting a GPX file on a phone
  • Strava: the app can't export. Open strava.com in your browser, switch to the desktop site, open the activity, then ⋯ → Export GPX.
  • Garmin Connect: on connect.garmin.com open the activity, then ⚙ → Export to GPX.
  • Most other apps (Komoot, Coros, Suunto, Runkeeper) have Share or Export → GPX. Save it to Files, then pick it here.

      How it works
      • Claim: close a loop (or finish within 75 m of your start) and everything inside becomes yours.
      • Paper.io move: run out of your land and back in, and the pocket in between is filled.
      • Steal: loop around someone else's land to take it.
      • Fade: land you don't visit for 14 days fades, and anyone can grab it just by running through. After 30 days it's neutral again.
      • Fair play: runs faster than 22 km/h on average, GPS teleports and planned routes (no timestamps) don't count.
      • The map is made of ~25 m hexagons, about the size of GPS error.

      🔒 Sandbox mode: your runs never leave this browser.
